1 · Before you book

  • My firm is in the Claude Partner NetworkCertification is open only to partner organizations.
  • I can sign in to the Anthropic Partner Academy with my company emailPersonal addresses do not work. A new email domain takes 7 to 10 days to be recognized, so ask early.
  • I am 18 or older, and my country is on the supported list
  • I picked the exam that matches my job, not the next one up the ladderThere are no prerequisites between exams.
  • I know whether I am testing before or after December 7And I downloaded the exam guide for that version.
  • The name in my exam profile matches my government ID exactlyLatin letters, middle name only if the ID shows it. It locks for a year once confirmed.
  • I checked my partner-tier discount shows at checkoutIt is applied automatically and also covers retakes.
  • I chose online (OnVUE) or a test centerIf my only network is corporate or VPN, a test center is the safer choice.
  • If I need accommodations or extra time, I requested them before schedulingAllow 10 days or more.

2 · Four-week study plan — our suggestion

  • Week 1: read the exam guide end to end and rate yourself on every domainThe guide is the authoritative scope. Mark the domains with the largest weights.
  • Week 1–2: take the free Claude Academy courses for your trackacademy.claude.com. Developer: Building with the Claude API, Tool use, Intro to MCP. Architect: MCP Advanced topics, Claude Code in action, subagents, agent skills.
  • Week 2–3: build one small project per weak domainThe questions are scenario-based. A tool interface you designed and an agent loop you debugged teach more than re-reading.
  • Week 3: take the partner prep courses in the Partner AcademyCoverage varies by exam.
  • Week 4: work the sample questions in the guide under timeThere is no official practice exam. Aim for under two minutes a question.
  • Week 4: write down, for each domain, the trade-off you would explain to a colleague

3 · Computer and network (a week before)

  • Windows 10 or macOS 14, or newerNo virtual machines or beta operating systems.
  • Working webcam, microphone and speakersNo headphones, headsets or earbuds.
  • One screen onlyDisconnect a second monitor; cover it if it cannot be removed.
  • At least 6 Mbps download and 2 Mbps upload
  • A personal networkVPNs, corporate networks and public or shared networks are not allowed.
  • I ran and passed Pearson's system test on this same computer and network
  • I can close every application except OnVUE

4 · Room and desk

  • A quiet room where I will be alone for the whole examNot an office, library, coffee shop or other public space.
  • Desk empty except the computerA drink in an unmarked container is allowed.
  • No books, notes, paper or pens within reachA digital whiteboard is provided in the exam.
  • Phone, tablet, watch and smart speakers out of reach
  • Whiteboards and note boards on the wall are wiped clean
  • Nobody else can see my screen

5 · ID

  • Valid, government-issued photo ID in handPassport, plastic driver's license or national ID card, for example.
  • It is the physical card, not expired, not a photo or digital copy
  • The name matches my exam booking exactly

6 · Exam day

  • Restart the computer; ask others at home not to stream
  • Start check-in 30 minutes before the appointmentUse the link in Pearson's confirmation email.
  • Check-in: technology checks, photos of me and my ID, a 360° room scan
  • Stay in the webcam view; do not read questions aloud
  • Do not touch my phone unless the proctor says so
  • If the computer freezes, relaunch OnVUE from the downloads folder
  • Need to move the exam? Online: up to one hour before. Test center: 48 hours before

7 · Afterwards

  • Note the score shown on screen at the end
  • Passed: claim the badge from the Credly emailAdd a personal email to Credly so the badge follows you between employers.
  • Not passed: read the per-section percentages in the score report
  • Retake waits: 14 days after the first attempt, 30 after the second, 90 after the thirdUp to four attempts per exam in any rolling 12 months.
  • Set a reminder for month 11: renewal is a free assessment if done on time
