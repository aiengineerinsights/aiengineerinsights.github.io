How to fill it in

  1. Use the numbers in the written offer, not the recruiter's summary of "total comp".
  2. Keep public and private stock apart. Public stock can be sold when it vests. Private stock cannot be sold until a tender offer or another liquidity event.
  3. Compare the four-year total first, then the four-year cash-only line. A large gap between the two means the offer depends on the stock.
  4. Compare like with like on location. San Francisco pays about $40K more than Austin in average base (page 1), before rent and state tax.

Questions to ask before you sign

  1. Which level is this offer at, and what is the band for that level?
  2. What is the vesting schedule and cliff? Is anything back-loaded?
  3. Are refresher grants standard, and how large is a typical one?
  4. For private stock: what price was the grant valued at, and when did employees last sell?
  5. What happens to unvested stock and the sign-on bonus if I leave in year one?
