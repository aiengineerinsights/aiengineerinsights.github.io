1 · The three books

Fundamentals of Software Architecture, 2eRichards, Ford. How to think like an architect.
Software Architecture: The Hard PartsFord, Richards, Sadalage, Dehghani. What breaks when systems are distributed.
AI EngineeringChip Huyen. What changes when a component is a model.

2 · In all three

Every choice is a trade-offName what you give up in every ADR
Fitness functions ≈ evalsAutomated checks in CI for the properties you care about
Orchestrated workflowsSagas and agent runs: who coordinates, what happens on failure
Schemas as contractsService contracts and structured model output
The bridge: an eval is a fitness function. If you gate releases on one, you already know how to do the other.
